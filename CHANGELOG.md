# Changelog

Every release of Duck, newest first. Backfilled from the commit history on
8 September 2026 — the version numbers below are the ones the commits
themselves name, so this is the real release history, not a reconstruction.

Duck was called Tuck until 1.4.0.

## [Unreleased]

## [1.5.0] - 2026-09-09
### Added
- What changed, in the window: the version in the foot opens the notes.
- Duck has a Homebrew list of its own.
- Duck says so when icons are out of its reach.

### Changed
- Duck's five items travel together.
- The mark carries the line.
- The line stands out past the icons.

## [1.4.2] - 2026-09-07
### Fixed
- The Dock tile follows dark mode, and so does the tab icon.

## [1.4.1] - 2026-09-07
### Changed
- The window is the approved layout: rows and a foot.

## [1.4.0] - 2026-09-06
### Changed
- A new name, a rubber duck, five marks. Tuck becomes Duck.
- The duck is drawn by hand, and the app icon is made from it.
- The duck is a black print, and everything that shows it is remade.
- Exposure in place of Fraunces, in the window title, the page headline and the pictures.

### Added
- Show in Dock, off by default.

### Removed
- Fraunces is gone; the font builder makes only Inter.

## [1.3.1] - 2026-09-05
### Changed
- One button on the page, and no welcome in the app.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-09-05
### Added

## [1.2.1] - 2026-09-05

## [1.2.0] - 2026-09-05
